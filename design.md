# KutumBari Restaurant - UI/UX Design System

## 1. Color Palette (Derived from Logo)
*   **Primary Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) - Keeps the minimal, clean look[cite: 7].
*   **Secondary Background/Hero:** Deep Forest Green - Adds the premium, luxury contrast[cite: 4].
*   **Primary Accent (Buttons/CTAs):** Vibrant Red - Draws the eye, matches the logo's serving pot and primary text.
*   **Secondary Accent (Hover states/Small text):** Warm Orange - Matches the logo's steam and secondary text.
*   **Text:** Dark Charcoal - For readable, crisp typography on white backgrounds[cite: 7].

## 2. Typography
*   **Headings:** Elegant Serif (e.g., Playfair Display) - Gives the premium, luxury restaurant vibe[cite: 6, 7].
*   **Body & UI Elements:** Clean Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter or Montserrat) - Ensures it is user-friendly and easy to read[cite: 4, 7].

## 3. Layout Structure

### A. Navigation Bar (Top)
*   **Style:** Transparent over the hero section, turning solid white on scroll.
*   **Elements:** Logo (left), simple links (Home, Menu, About, Contact) (center), "Book a Table" Red CTA button (right)[cite: 4, 7].

### B. Hero Section
*   **Visual:** Deep green background[cite: 4] with a high-quality, floating 3D-style dish image on the right[cite: 6].
*   **Text (Left):** "স্বাদের সাথে ভালোবাসার এক আপন ঠিকানা" (tagline) in clean white text.
*   **Action:** Two buttons - "Explore Menu" (Red) and "Our Story" (Outline)[cite: 4].

### C. Trust/Feature Badges (Below Hero)
*   **Layout:** A simple horizontal row of minimalist icons[cite: 4, 7].
*   **Content:** "Fresh Ingredients," "Expert Chefs," "Cozy Ambience," "Event Hosting"[cite: 4, 7].

### D. Menu Section
*   **Categories:** Circular thumbnail images for Bangla, Chinese, and Fast Food categories[cite: 7].
*   **Cards:** Clean white cards with a subtle shadow, high-quality dish image, title, brief description, and price[cite: 4, 7]. Keep spacing tight but breathable to avoid complications.

### E. Location & Contact
*   **Visual:** Clean white background featuring an embedded map or minimalist location graphic for Bhandaria[cite: 5].
*   **Text:** Display the Reserve Pukur Par address and the two contact numbers clearly.

### F. Footer
*   **Style:** Dark green background to close out the page securely[cite: 4, 5].
*   **Content:** Minimal logo, quick links, lunch/dinner service hours, and social media icons[cite: 4].
